# OneB Market Vision

OneB Market is a trading decision intelligence platform. Its job is not to
promise buy/sell certainty; it helps a trader understand evidence, regime,
risk, uncertainty, invalidation and learning opportunities before acting.

Core product question:

```text
Given this market regime, this asset, this user profile and this data quality,
is there enough evidence to make a decision?
```

Preferred answers include `NO_TRADE`. Abstaining when evidence, calibration or
data quality is weak is a product feature, not a failure.

## Product Pillars

- Intelligence: structured signals, regime, evidence and uncertainty.
- Research: reproducible experiments with dataset and model versions.
- Risk: portfolio-aware guardrails before any execution path.
- Copilot: explanation, synthesis, journaling and education.
- Education: lessons triggered by real decision gaps and trader behavior.

## Positioning

OneB Market is not trying to be Bloomberg, MetaTrader, Robinhood or a generic
course platform. The opportunity is the intersection of market monitoring,
decision support, research discipline, risk control and trader education.
